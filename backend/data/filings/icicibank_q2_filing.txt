ICICI Bank Q2 FY26 Earnings Summary (Excerpt)

Net interest income grew 9.8% YoY, ahead of sector peers, driven by strong
retail and SME loan book growth. The cost-to-income ratio improved further as
digital channels now account for the large majority of transaction volumes.
Gross NPA ratio stood at 1.9%, broadly stable sequentially, with provisioning
coverage maintained above management's internal floor.

Management highlighted continued momentum in unsecured retail lending,
alongside disciplined underwriting standards, as a key driver of margin
resilience. Deposit growth kept pace with credit growth, keeping the
loan-to-deposit ratio within the target band.

Risk Factors: Rising competitive intensity in unsecured retail lending and
potential asset-quality drift if underwriting standards loosen sector-wide
were flagged as items to monitor.
