Infosys Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)

CFO Remarks: Revenue grew 4.1% YoY in constant currency, at the lower end of
the guided range as discretionary technology spending among North American
clients stayed muted. Operating margin held at 21.2%, supported by continued
utilization gains and a weaker rupee. Large deal wins totaled $2.3B for the
quarter, with a healthy mix of cost-takeout and vendor-consolidation deals.

Management narrowed full-year revenue growth guidance downward, citing macro
uncertainty in the BFSI and retail verticals, while noting early signs of
recovery in manufacturing and communications clients. Attrition eased to
13.4%, the lowest in three quarters.

Risk Factors: Continued caution on discretionary IT budgets among key
enterprise clients, and pricing pressure from AI-led productivity expectations
in renewal negotiations, were noted as items to monitor.
