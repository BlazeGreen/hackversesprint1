HDFC Bank Q2 FY26 Earnings Summary (Excerpt)

Net interest income grew 8.1% YoY. Deposit growth outpaced credit growth this quarter,
improving the loan-to-deposit ratio toward management's target range. Gross NPA ratio
remained stable at 1.3%, among the lowest in the sector.

Management commentary emphasized a "steady, low-drama" quarter, with no major surprises
on asset quality or provisioning. The bank continued its branch expansion into semi-urban
markets as part of its multi-year deposit-mobilization strategy.

Risk Factors: Margin pressure from deposit repricing in a competitive rate environment,
and integration-related costs from prior merger activities, were noted as ongoing items
to monitor.
