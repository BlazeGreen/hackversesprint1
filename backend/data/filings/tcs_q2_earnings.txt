TCS Q2 FY26 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)

CFO Remarks: Revenue grew 6.2% YoY in constant currency terms, driven by strong deal wins
in BFSI and cloud transformation segments. Operating margin held steady at 24.3%, within
guided range. Attrition remained low at 12.1%, the lowest in eight quarters.

Management flagged continued caution in discretionary IT spending among North American
clients, but noted European deal pipeline strength, including a large multi-year cloud
migration contract signed this quarter. Management reiterated full-year revenue growth
guidance of 5-7% in constant currency.

Risk Factors: Currency volatility, wage inflation in select geographies, and prolonged
client budget cycles in the technology and retail verticals were cited as near-term
watch items.
