Reliance Industries Q2 FY26 Earnings Summary (Excerpt)

Consolidated EBITDA grew 9.4% YoY, led by continued subscriber additions and
ARPU improvement at Jio and robust footfall growth at Reliance Retail. Oil-to-
chemicals segment margins came under pressure as refining cracks normalized
from last year's elevated levels, partially offsetting gains elsewhere.

Management reiterated its capex guidance for new energy (green hydrogen and
battery storage) manufacturing capacity, with first commissioning expected
over the next few quarters. Retail segment same-store sales growth remained
in double digits for the fourth consecutive quarter.

Risk Factors: Continued softness in refining margins if crack spreads stay
compressed, and the scale of new energy capex relative to near-term free cash
flow generation, were flagged as items to monitor.
