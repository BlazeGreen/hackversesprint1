Zomato Ltd. Q2 FY26 Regulatory Filing Excerpt (SEBI Disclosure)

The Company reports a narrowing but persistent EBITDA margin compression in its
quick-commerce segment (Blinkit) due to elevated dark-store expansion costs and
competitive discounting pressure from new entrants in the sector.

Food delivery GOV (Gross Order Value) grew 14% YoY, though take-rate remained flat
sequentially. Management stated it expects quick-commerce segment losses to peak
in the next two quarters before improving as dark-store utilization matures.

A promoter-group entity disclosed a stake sale of 1.2% of total equity during the
quarter, stated to be for "personal liquidity purposes," per the exchange disclosure
filed under SEBI (SAST) Regulations.

Risk Factors: Intensifying competition in quick-commerce, regulatory scrutiny of gig
worker classification, and continued cash burn in new segments were disclosed as
material risks.
